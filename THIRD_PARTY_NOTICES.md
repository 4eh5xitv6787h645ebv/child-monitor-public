# Third-party notices

## Brahms Lullaby

`app/src/main/res/raw/brahms_lullaby_elsie_baker.ogg` is the English-language vocal
recording “Lullaby” (*Wiegenlied*, Op. 49 No. 4), performed by Elsie Baker and
published as Victor 17181 in 1912. It was downloaded from this YouTube publication:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hv2hItS1HpE

The Library of Congress catalogues the same 1912 recording here:

https://www.loc.gov/item/jukebox-132112/

The recording was published before 1923 and is in the public domain. The bundled file is an
audio-only, mono Ogg Vorbis transcode made for Child Monitor. The musical composition,
Johannes Brahms’ *Wiegenlied*, Op. 49 No. 4, is also in the public domain.
